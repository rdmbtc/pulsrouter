The Promise

Nanopayments shouldn't need a payments team.

Paid APIs are exploding across the x402 ecosystem — and every one of them threatens to make you implement 402 handshakes, price comparison, and spend control yourself. Don't.

01
The PainEvery paid API invents its own payment scheme. Headers here, tokens there, SDKs everywhere.
The FixOne request shape forever: POST /proxy {"type","q"}. The HTTP 402 dance — terms, signature, settlement — is ours, not yours.
02
The PainPrice and uptime are a lottery you play blind. Today's cheapest provider is tomorrow's 500 error.
The FixEvery call, PulsRouter sorts the catalog cheapest-first, skips unhealthy nodes, and walks down the list until someone answers.
03
The PainAutonomous agents don't know when to stop spending. A retry loop can drain a wallet while you sleep.
The FixDaily USDC caps per data type, checked before any offer is made. An exhausted budget routes nowhere — by construction.
How It Works

Your app → PulsRouter → the provider. That's the whole picture.

One POST in, receipt plus payload out. Between those two events, four things happen in milliseconds — none of them your problem.

Catalog

Config localRows and registry.json merge with Circle x402 Discovery, filtered to your chain, deduped.

Route

Filter by type, sort by price, enforce the daily budget. Cheapest healthy candidate first.

Pay

The winner gets an x402 handshake. USDC leaves your agent wallet only after terms are accepted.

Receipt

{via, priceUsdc, result} returns to your app. Failures fall through to the next candidate automatically.

If the winner won't answer, the runner-up already has your query.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Is this real money?
Yes — real USDC settlement through the x402 flow, via your Circle Agent Wallet. Everything defaults to ARC-TESTNET, so you can run the entire pipeline on test funds before touching mainnet. Daily budget caps keep the blast radius small either way.
What do I need installed?
Node ≥ 18, the Circle CLI logged in (circle wallet status), and a wallet with a little USDC. That's it — PulsRouter has zero npm dependencies and no build step. Clone, init, set your wallet, pay.
Can I use my own providers?
Yes. Any x402 resource works: add four fields to pulsrouter.config.json — type, name, endpoint, priceUsdc — and it joins the catalog alongside Discovery results. Your local rows win endpoint duplicates, and nothing about your catalog ever leaves your box.
